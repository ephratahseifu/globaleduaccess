Application Instructions & Required Documents

High School Level (Grades 9 - 12) Requirements

  1. Academic Transcript: Certified grade reports for Grades 8 through current term.
  2. Valid Passport: Clear scan of bio-page (Minimum 6 months validity).
  3. Recommendation Letter: Written recommendation from School Principal or Teacher.
  4. English Proficiency Test: TOEFL Junior, IELTS, Duolingo, or Official School Medium of Instruction Letter.
  5. Medical Certificate: General physical fitness report certified by a recognized physician.
